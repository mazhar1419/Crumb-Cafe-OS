# Product Overview

## Summary

Crumb Cafe OS is a web-based cafe operations product combining a cafe-specific public storefront with an authenticated workspace for point-of-sale and back-office tasks. The supplied source describes it as a multi-tenant cafe website and POS SaaS.

**Live application:** https://crumb.theabacuses.com/

## The problem

Small cafes often split everyday work across a till, handwritten notes, customer phone lists, menu updates, and separate expense records. That creates duplicated work and makes it harder to trace a customer order from the storefront or counter to its final status and reporting.

Crumb brings several of those workflows into one cafe workspace.

## Intended users

- Independent cafe and coffee-shop owners
- Small teams managing a menu and daily orders
- Cafe operators who need basic customer, expense, and sales visibility

## Core workflow

1. An owner registers a cafe workspace.
2. The owner configures menu categories and items.
3. The cafe can use its POS screen for in-person sales.
4. Customers can browse the cafe-specific storefront and submit an order request.
5. Staff review orders and update their status and payment records.
6. The owner reviews customers, expenses, and date-filtered reports.

## Product principles

- **Operational clarity:** keep core daily workflows close together.
- **Cafe-level data separation:** associate operational records with a cafe organization.
- **Accessible UI:** use a responsive browser-based interface rather than requiring a dedicated desktop installation.
- **Practical scope:** represent order/payment state honestly; do not imply online payment processing where no gateway is configured.

## Current scope

The reviewed package includes POS sales, menu categories/items, cafe storefronts, website order requests, order management, customer records, expenses, reports, and cafe settings. It should be described as an evolving SaaS-style product, not as a fully audited enterprise platform.
